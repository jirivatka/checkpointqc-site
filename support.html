Reporting a problem

Delivery files are all different, and the useful detail is usually in the specifics. When something doesn't behave, include:

  • The delivery spec you were checking against, and the operator it belongs to.
  • The file: container, codec, raster, frame rate and audio layout — the app's metadata read-out has all of it, and Copy puts it on the clipboard as text.
  • What you expected the rule to do, and what it did instead.
  • Your macOS version and Mac model.

Please don't upload client media. The metadata read-out is nearly always enough, and if it isn't, a short synthetic clip that reproduces the behaviour is better for everyone.

Reporting a wrong spec

Specifications change, and operators revise them without much fanfare. If a rule looks wrong, say so — point at the document. Every rule in the app cites the published source it was transcribed from, along with the version and the date it was checked, so a report that names the current document and the clause it contradicts can be fixed quickly and confidently.

Spec corrections are the most valuable reports this project gets.

Requirements

  • macOS 14 or later.
  • Apple silicon and Intel Macs — the app ships as a universal binary.
  • Mac only. There is no iPad version: verification reads real deliverables off local and network storage, and an iPad has no honest story for that.

What you get without paying

The whole catalogue is browsable for free — every spec, every rule, and the source citation behind it. File verification is enabled free on two open industry standards, AS-11 UK DPP HD and EBU R 128 Programme, so you can point the app at a real master and see what it actually does before deciding anything. PDF QC report export is part of Full Unlock.

Full Unlock is a one-time purchase that enables verification and PDF export across the whole catalogue, and keeps every spec that shipped with your version — permanently.

Purchases and restoring

Purchases go through the App Store. If you've reinstalled, moved to a new Mac, or signed in with a different Apple Account, use Restore on the unlock screen — it re-checks your entitlements with Apple and doesn't charge you again.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Spec Updates, and what happens if it lapses

Letting the subscription lapse never takes anything away. Every spec that shipped with the version you bought stays unlocked, forever. The subscription only adds specifications published after your purchase.

It's optional, and it's meant to be. Buy once and the app keeps working exactly as it did the day you bought it.

How accurate are the specs?

Every rule is transcribed from an operator's own published documentation and cites it, with the document version and the date it was verified. Where a specification is genuinely ambiguous, the app says so rather than inventing a tolerance.

That said: operators revise their documents, and your delivery contract is the authority, not this app. Treat Checkpoint QC as a fast, thorough second pair of eyes — and verify against the current document before hand-off.

Your files stay on your Mac

Verification, loudness measurement and black/freeze detection all run locally. Media is never uploaded. See the privacy policy for the detail.
